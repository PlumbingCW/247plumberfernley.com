A tankless water heater heats water only when a tap is open, so it does not keep a tank of hot water warm all day. Call the 24/7 line if yours has failed or you want to plan a switch, and we will explain the requirements and give a clear price before work starts.

Tankless water heater service in Fernley covers new installs, replacements for older tank units, repair of error codes and descaling. These units need more planning than a tank, because gas line size, electric capacity and venting all have to match the model.

Requirements

What a tankless unit needs to work well

These three items make or break a tankless install.

Gas or electric capacity

A gas tankless unit burns a lot of fuel at once. A standard residential gas line can be too small, so a larger line may be needed. An electric unit can need a heavy circuit and panel capacity.

Venting

Gas units need approved venting that is sized and sloped for the model. A unit in a garage or closet has different needs than one on an outside wall.

Flow rate and incoming water

The unit has a maximum flow rate and a temperature rise limit. Cold incoming water in winter lowers how much hot water the unit can deliver at once.

Service

Tankless repair and maintenance

Tankless units are reliable when maintained. These are the tasks we handle.

Error codes

Many units show a code for ignition, flame, exhaust or flow faults. We read the code and test the cause.

Scale buildup

Mineral deposits narrow the heat exchanger. A descaling flush is the usual maintenance.

Cold water sandwich

A short blast of cold water between hot bursts is a known tankless trait. We discuss fixes such as recirculation.

Venting and condensate

Condensing models drain acidic condensate. The drain and neutralizer need to stay clear.

Local conditions

Water quality and tankless service in Fernley

Mineral buildup affects tankless heat exchangers, and a water softener or a regular descaling flush can help. We do not quote a hardness number for Fernley, because it varies by source and neighborhood. To learn what is in your water, ask the City of Fernley Utility Billing office at 775-784-9930 for the annual Consumer Confidence Report.

If you want to treat your water, see water softener and filtration for options. Bring the report to your appointment and we can talk about what it means for a tankless unit.

Permits and code

According to the Nevada State Contractors Board, permits and inspections are required for new and replacement water heaters, and this includes tankless units. We handle the details with you. The City of Fernley Building Department can be reached at 775-784-9900.

For other water heater types, see water heater installation.

Cost factors

What affects the price of a tankless install

The unit model, gas or electric upgrades, venting, location and permit all affect the price. A straight swap on an existing wall is simpler than a new gas line and vent.

You get a clear price before work starts, and we itemize upgrades so you can see what they add.
