Hydro jetting is the call to make when a drain backs up again and again, or when a main line stops completely and the cable stalls. Our line is open 24/7, and while you wait you should stop using water, keep drains closed and tell the dispatcher what a cable has already done.

Hydro jetting in Fernley uses a hose with a rotating nozzle that sends water backward against the pipe wall at high pressure. It scours grease, scale and soft debris from the inside of the pipe instead of only boring a hole through the blockage.

The problem

Why a cable alone sometimes fails

Drain machines and jets solve different problems, and picking the wrong one is how a line gets cleared and then clogs again.

The cable opens a channel

A cable cutter spins through the blockage and leaves a hole about the size of the cutting head. Water flows again, but the grease and scale on the pipe wall stay in place.

Buildup closes the channel

Grease re-coats the opening, scale narrows it and the clog returns. Some customers describe snaking the same line every few weeks for a year.

Jetting removes the coating

A jet nozzle washes the whole circumference of the pipe, so the line returns closer to its full diameter. That is the difference people notice most.

Urgent situations

Backups that justify an emergency jetting call

Jetting is a planned maintenance tool as well as an emergency tool. These four situations push it to the front of the queue.

Main line stops draining

When a cable stalls or pulls back only grease, the line needs a stronger approach. Stop using water in the house.

Sewage in a floor drain or tub

Wastewater coming up at the lowest drain is a health risk. Keep people away and call the 24/7 line.

Restaurant or shop floor drains overflow

A food-service line that backs up during service can close a business for the day. See commercial plumbing for after-hours work.

The previous snaking got stuck

A cable that jams in a line can point to a break, a root mass or a hard deposit. A camera look comes before the jet.

Process

How a jetting visit runs

The steps are the same each time. The order matters because a jet is the wrong tool for a collapsed pipe.

Camera first

We run a camera down the line to see the blockage, the pipe material and any breaks. Jetting a broken pipe can make it worse.

Jet the line

A nozzle on a flexible hose travels the length of the problem section while the water scours the walls.

Camera again

A second pass confirms the pipe is clear and shows what shape the walls are in.

Review and advice

We explain what we found and whether the line needs a repair, a maintenance schedule or nothing further.

Pipes that should not be jetted

Hydro jetting is a good fit for sound pipe with buildup. It is not the right choice for pipe that is cracked, collapsed, or badly offset at a joint. A camera look decides this before we start.

Older cast iron with heavy scale can be jetted, but the scale flakes off in pieces and has to be caught and removed. We tell you what we see on the screen and whether a lining or a section repair makes more sense. See sewer line repair if the camera shows damage.

Jetting for Fernley homes and businesses

Fernley has homes built across several decades, so drain pipe material varies from one street to the next. Plastic lines jet cleanly, while older cast iron or clay can carry scale and need the camera check first.

Businesses along Main Street and warehouses in the Victory Logistics District have larger drains and floor drains that collect wash water and grease. The right pressure depends on pipe size and material, so the camera comes first. A jetting plan for a business is set up on site, with a clear price before work starts.

Cost factors

What affects the price of hydro jetting

The price depends on the length of line, the diameter of the pipe, the amount of buildup and whether the line has to be cleared by cable first. Access also matters, since a clean main cleanout lets the hose run straight in.

After-hours calls are priced for the extra dispatch. We give you the number up front so you can decide before work begins, and we tell you if a repair is a better use of money than another cleaning.
