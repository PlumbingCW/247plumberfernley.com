Urgent situations

What to do when a business plumbing line fails

A fast response limits both damage and downtime.

Find the right shutoff

Know where the building main and each branch valve are. A restroom group often has its own shutoff.

Protect people

Block off wet floors, and keep staff away from areas where sewage has backed up.

Protect inventory

Move stock, paper goods and electronics off the floor. Photograph conditions for the insurance claim.

Local sites

Fernley commercial sites and what they need

Fernley sits along I-80 and US-50A, and the Victory Logistics District is a 4,300-acre district near Duffy Road and Nevada Pacific Parkway. Distribution and light industrial buildings there use large floor drains and restroom groups that see heavy use around shift changes.

Older buildings and food-service sites carry grease and aging pipe risks, so we plan a camera check for those. For after-hours work, we coordinate with the property manager when one is involved.

Permits, backflow and code

Commercial plumbing often needs permits and inspections that residential repairs do not. The City of Fernley Building Department and Code Enforcement is at 775-784-9900 on weekdays and has an Online Permit Center. We explain what applies to your job.

Some commercial properties have backflow preventers that need testing on a schedule. Ask us or your water utility what your site requires.
