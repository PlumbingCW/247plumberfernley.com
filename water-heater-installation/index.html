Water heater installation in Fernley covers new homes, additions, remodels, garage conversions and changes from one fuel type to another. The work follows the manufacturer's instructions and local code, and permits and inspections are part of the plan.

Planning

Planning a new water heater installation

The best installs are planned. A few questions up front prevent changes on the day.

Pick the location

Garages, closets, attics and utility rooms each have clearance, venting and drain requirements. A gas unit needs a flue path, and an electric one needs a circuit.

Choose the type

Storage, tankless and hybrid heaters have different space, power and cost needs. We help match the choice to your household.

Check utilities

A larger gas appliance may need a bigger gas line. An electric unit may need a new breaker. We check before ordering.

What a complete installation includes

A tidy installation covers all of these.

  • Proper support and a drain pan with a pipe to a safe discharge point where required.
  • A temperature and pressure relief valve with a discharge line routed per code.
  • Shutoff valves on the cold supply and, when practical, on hot lines.
  • Gas piping with a sediment trap and a leak test on gas units.
  • Venting that is sized and sloped for the unit, checked for draft.
  • An expansion tank when the system needs one.
  • A permit and inspection arranged for the work.

If anything on this list does not apply, we say why.

Permits and inspections

According to the Nevada State Contractors Board, new and replacement water heaters require permits and inspections. We plan around that, and we explain how the City of Fernley handles it. The Building Department and Code Enforcement office is at City Hall, 595 Silver Lace Blvd, and its phone is 775-784-9900.

For an emergency swap, see water heater replacement. For a tankless install in particular, see tankless water heater.

Local conditions

Installing in a garage or outbuilding

Many garage installs need an elevated base for gas units so ignition sources sit above the floor, along with protection from vehicle impact. Fernley's winters can drop a garage well below freezing, so insulation on piping and the tank is worth considering.

If you are converting a garage or building an addition, mention it when you call. We plan supply, drain and vent routes before walls are closed. A plumbing installation visit can cover other new fixtures at the same time.

Cost factors

What affects the price of installation

The price depends on the unit, location, fuel, venting, any gas or electrical changes and the permit. A straightforward swap in the same location costs less than a new location.

You get a clear price before work starts. We separate the unit, labor and permit so you can see what each piece costs.
