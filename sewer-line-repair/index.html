Sewage backing into a tub or floor drain is a health hazard, so treat it as an emergency. Our line is open 24/7. While you wait, stop using water, keep people away from the wet area and do not flush toilets.

Sewer line repair in Fernley starts with a camera that shows where the pipe is cracked, offset, root-filled or collapsed, so the fix is sized to the damage. A short spot repair and a full replacement are very different jobs, and the camera is how we tell them apart before we dig.

Urgent situations

Sewer backup: what to do first

The first hour sets how much damage follows. These steps are safe for anyone to take while the plumber is on the way.

Stop adding water

Pause the washer, dishwasher and showers. Every gallon you add has to go somewhere, and the lowest drain is where it surfaces.

Do not use chemical openers

They rarely clear a main line and they leave caustic water for the plumber and your family to deal with.

Protect the area

Keep children and pets out. Lift rugs and valuables onto higher ground. Photograph the water level for your records.

Check the outside cleanout

If you can safely see a capped pipe near the house, do not open it. Raw sewage can flow out. Tell the dispatcher where it is.

Diagnosis

How a camera finds the problem

A sewer line repair should never start with a guess. The camera result sets the plan and the price.

Locating the break

A camera head on a flexible cable travels the line from a cleanout. A transmitter in the head lets the tech mark the exact spot and depth at the surface.

Reading what the pipe shows

Cracks, offset joints and low spots each look different. Roots show as fine fibers or a solid mass, and scale shows as rough pipe wall.

Sorting repair from cleaning

A line with a clog but sound pipe needs sewer line cleaning. A damaged line needs a repair. The camera is what separates the two.

Which option fits depends on what the camera shows, on soil and on how deep and long the line is.

Cast iron and older pipe

Older homes can have cast iron drain lines that rust from the inside out. The pipe wall flakes, the diameter narrows and a clog follows. Cast iron pipe repair usually means replacing the failed section with modern plastic pipe and joining it with couplings made for the job.

We show you the camera footage and the pipe sample when it is practical. If most of the run is in similar shape, we talk through replacement and spell out the price before work starts. See repiping if supply pipes in the same house are also from that era.

Septic and sewer: know which system you have

Most homes inside Fernley are on the city sewer, and the City of Fernley manages sewer accounts through its Utility Billing office. Rural properties and some outlying parcels use septic tanks, and in Silver Springs the sewer service is limited to certain areas.

A septic line that backs up needs a different plan than a city lateral. We can tell which you have from the cleanout layout and the camera. Properties south of Fernley are covered on our Silver Springs emergency plumber page, where wells and septic are more common.

Local conditions

Roots, ground movement and the Truckee Canal

Roots enter through joints that have shifted, and in a cold desert climate the soil cycles between frozen and thawed in winter. That movement can pull joints apart over the years. Fernley's soil moisture also depends on irrigation and the Truckee Canal system, which can raise the water table near the canal.

A high water table works against a sewer line because groundwater can enter through cracks. If your yard stays wet near the line, or you see sewage odors outdoors, mention it when you call. A camera look is the way to tell a leak from simple irrigation runoff.

Cost factors

What affects the price of sewer line repair

The main factors are the depth and length of the damaged section, the repair method, surface restoration and any permit fees. A lining job and an open trench job for the same house can land far apart in cost.

You get a clear price before work starts, and we explain what is and is not included. If a section repair would leave other weak areas in place, we say so and let you decide.
