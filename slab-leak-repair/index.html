Slab leak repair in Fernley starts with pinpointing the leak without breaking up the whole floor. Once we know where it is and why, you can choose between a spot repair in the slab, a reroute that carries water around the damaged section or a repipe of the affected lines.

Urgent situations

Signs of a slab leak

A slab leak hides until it does damage. These four signs are the ones to watch for.

A warm spot on the floor

A hot water line leaking under concrete can heat the surface above it. Barefoot, you may feel a warm patch.

The sound of running water

Hearing water with every fixture off points to a pressurized leak. Put an ear near the floor and listen.

Wet carpet or buckling floors

Moisture rising through the slab lifts flooring and stains baseboards. Mold can follow within days.

An unexplained bill increase

If the meter moves with everything off, the leak could be under the slab or in the yard. A pressure test sorts it out.

Locating

How the leak gets located

The same tools used in leak detection apply here, with extra attention to depth and layout.

Isolating the line

We test hot and cold lines separately. A pressure drop in one tells us which circuit to follow.

Pinpointing the spot

Acoustic equipment and thermal imaging mark the location on the floor. Marking it avoids guessing and limits the area opened.

Checking the cause

Corrosion, a bad fitting, a pipe rubbing on concrete and ground movement each leave a different mark. The cause shapes the repair choice.

Why a slab leak is worth acting on quickly

Water that escapes under a slab can wash soil away, damage flooring and feed mold. The longer it runs, the more it costs to dry out and repair. A leak that seems small can still waste a lot of water over a month.

If you cannot tell whether the leak is under the slab or elsewhere, shut off the main and call. We begin with a meter test and a pressure test so you know where the water is going.

Local conditions

Fernley homes and slab foundations

If your Fernley home sits on a concrete slab, some supply lines may run in or under the concrete. Pipes can wear against the slab over time, and the cold desert climate cycles soil moisture and temperature across the year.

Fernley's history includes a flood from the Truckee Canal levee breach in January 2008. That event is a reminder that ground water matters here. If your yard or slab edge stays damp for no clear reason, tell the plumber. The City of Fernley Building Department at 775-784-9900 can advise on permit needs for repairs and reroutes.

Cost factors

What affects the price of slab leak repair

The price depends on the number of leaks, the repair method, the depth of the pipe and the flooring on top. A reroute through an attic costs differently from a tunnel under a slab.

You get a clear price before work starts. If a permit applies, we point you to the right City office, and we explain what is covered. Insurance coverage for slab leaks varies, so ask your insurer what documentation they want.
