What we do and how we work

Fernley 24/7 Plumbing is a plumbing service that answers the phone every hour of the day. You call, describe the problem and a licensed plumber is scheduled. The goal is simple: a clear price before work starts and a plumber who explains what is wrong in plain language.

We handle emergency and planned work, from drain clearing and burst pipes to water heater replacement and repiping. Our shop is at 460 W Main St in Fernley, Nevada 89408.

How we think about trust

Plumbing is a service most people buy under stress, so we try to make the process easy to check. We confirm the price before work starts. We tell you when a repair makes sense and when replacement is the better choice. We say when a problem is outside our scope.

We do not post invented reviews or ratings on this site. If you want to check a plumber's license, the Nevada State Contractors Board has a public license lookup, and you can ask us about our license on the phone.

Where we work

We serve Fernley and Silver Springs, and we can talk about Wadsworth, Hazen and Patrick when you call. For details on the Silver Springs area, see our Silver Springs plumber page.

For problems we see most often, start with drain cleaning, water heater repair or leak detection. The full list of services is on our services page.

Process

What a typical job looks like

Every visit follows the same four steps, whether it is a clogged sink or a failed water heater.

The call

You describe the problem, the dispatcher notes your address and what you have already done, and a time frame is set. If water is running, the first instruction is to shut off the main valve.

The visit

The plumber looks at the problem, tests it and explains what is wrong in plain words. If the fix is simple, you hear that. If it is bigger, you hear that too, with the options.

The price

You hear the price before work starts. If the scope changes after the plumber opens a wall or a line, you hear the new number before we continue.

The follow-up

After the work, we explain what was done and what to watch for. Keep the invoice and any permit paperwork for your records.

Scope

Services we offer

Our work falls into four groups. Drains and sewers include drain cleaning, hydro jetting, sewer line repair, sewer line cleaning and sewer camera inspection. Water heaters include repair, replacement, installation and tankless units.

Leaks and pipes include leak detection, slab leak repair, burst pipe repair and repiping. Fixtures and business work include toilets, faucets, disposals, commercial plumbing and water softener and filtration.

Local context

Working in Fernley and Lyon County

Fernley incorporated in 2001 and sits in Lyon County, with 22,895 residents at the 2020 census. It has a cold desert climate, with January lows that average about 23 degrees and July highs near 94. That climate shapes our work: freeze damage, water heaters in cold garages and exposed lines in older buildings.

The city runs its own water and sewer billing through Utility Billing at 775-784-9930, and permits through the Building Department at 775-784-9900. We tell you which office to ask when a job needs one, and we explain the Nevada contractor rules that apply.

Trust

Licensing and what we will not claim

Plumbing and heating work in Nevada falls under the C-1 classification of the Nevada State Contractors Board, and the board requires permits and inspections for new and replacement water heaters. You can verify any contractor through the board public lookup.

We do not publish review counts, star ratings, awards or years in business on this site, because we want every claim here to be something you can check. If you want to know more about our license or insurance, ask on the phone.

Our approach

When the honest answer is no

Sometimes the right advice is to do less. A running toilet may need a ten dollar part instead of a new fixture. A slow drain may only need a hand tool. A water heater with a failed element may be worth repairing, even though a replacement sale would be larger.

We would rather explain the real options and let you choose. If a job is outside what we do, such as a well drilling problem or an electrical panel upgrade, we say so and point you toward the right trade.
