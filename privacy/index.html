Privacy Policy

What information we collect when you call, email or visit this website, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you call (855) 575-2890, we collect the phone number you call from, the address of the job and the details you give about the problem. Calls may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you email us, we receive your email address and your message.

This website does not have forms and does not ask you to create an account.

How we use information

We use call and email information to schedule and complete plumbing work, to follow up on a visit and to improve our service. We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with people who help complete the job, such as a parts supplier or a permit office, and where the law requires it.

Website technology

This website includes embedded Google Maps. When a map loads, Google may collect information as described in its own privacy policy. Our web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and pages requested, for security and reliability.

If we add analytics in the future, we will update this page.

Your choices and contact

You can ask us to delete or correct information we hold about you by emailing contact@247plumberfernley.com or calling the main line. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call.

This policy may change, and the date on this page shows the latest update. It was last updated on November 16, 2026.
