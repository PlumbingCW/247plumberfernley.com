Water softener and filtration work in Fernley starts with knowing what is in your water. We do not state a hardness number for the town, because it varies by source and neighborhood. We recommend testing your tap and asking your water utility for its latest report.

First step

Start with the test, not the product

A home test kit or a lab test tells you hardness, and a utility report tells you what is in the supply. A softener removes calcium and magnesium. It does not remove chlorine, odors or most contaminants, which need filtration.

The City of Fernley Utility Billing office at 775-784-9930 manages water accounts and lists annual Consumer Confidence Reports. Rural wells have their own testing needs, and well owners should test with a certified lab.

Options

Types of systems

Each system has its place, and many homes use a combination.

Salt-based softener

Exchanges hardness minerals for sodium or potassium. Needs salt and a drain connection.

Whole-house carbon filter

Reduces chlorine taste and odor for every tap.

Under-sink filter

Treats drinking and cooking water at one tap. Easy to install, small footprint.

Reverse osmosis

A multi-stage filter for drinking water, with a small faucet at the sink.

Softeners and the rest of your plumbing

A softener adds a drain line and a salt supply. The water heater, tankless unit and fixtures all benefit from softer water, because scale builds up less. Ask about the tankless link at tankless water heater.

The system should be installed with a bypass valve and a proper air gap on the drain line. We install both, and we check the existing supply lines before connecting.

Maintenance

Service and maintenance

A salt-based softener needs salt and periodic cleaning of the brine tank. Filters need new cartridges on a schedule set by the manufacturer. An unmaintained system can lose effectiveness or even add problems.

We service common brands and explain the schedule for yours. If a system leaks or fails, call the 24/7 line. For hidden leaks, see leak detection.

What affects the price of a softener or filter

The price depends on the system type and size, the layout of the plumbing, the drain and power access and the permit if one applies.
