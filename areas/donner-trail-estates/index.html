Overview

Plumbing help for a newer Fernley subdivision

Donner Trail Estates is a single-family subdivision in Fernley, Nevada 89408. Real estate sites place it on the western side of town and describe construction that began in the 2010s and continued in phases. One listing service labels homes by phase, with numbers such as Phase 8.

If you own a home here, you probably live in a newer house than much of the city. That changes the conversation. The questions are about warranties, setup and routine care more than about old pipes.

Do this first

A first walkthrough of your home's plumbing

Whether the home is new to you or new to the market, a short walkthrough pays off.

Find the main shutoff

Locate the main water valve and turn it a quarter turn to check that it moves. Label it so anyone in the house can find it fast.

Read the water heater label

The tank label shows the size, the fuel and often the manufacture date. Take a photo and keep it with your warranty papers.

Check every outdoor faucet

Look for drips and learn where each hose bib shuts off inside the house. Disconnect hoses before the first hard freeze.

Run each fixture once

Flush the toilets, run the sinks and showers and look under cabinets for damp spots. Small leaks are easier to fix when they are new.

Before you hire

Builder warranty and when to call us

If your home is still under a builder warranty, read its terms before you hire someone for a covered item. Work done by another company can affect coverage, and the builder may require its own contractors.

For anything the warranty does not cover, or in an emergency such as a burst line, call us and we will help you stop the damage first. Then you can decide who completes the repair.

City offices

Permits, accounts and city contacts

New water heaters and some other jobs can need permits. For permits, the City of Fernley Building Department answers at 775-784-9900 on weekdays. Water and sewer accounts are handled by Utility Billing at 775-784-9930.

Your plumber explains what applies to your job before work starts. If you are unsure whether a repair involves a permit, ask the Building Department first.
