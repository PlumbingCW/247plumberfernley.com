Fernley 24/7 Plumbing is based at 460 W Main St in Fernley, Nevada, and dispatches along Interstate 80, US 50 Alternate and US 95A. Calls inside Fernley are our most common, and the whole city is covered by the same 24/7 line shown on every page.

Outside the city, we have a dedicated page for Silver Springs, about 15 miles south in Lyon County. Smaller communities nearby, such as Wadsworth, Hazen and Patrick, are also within reach. Call and give the address, and we will confirm whether we can come and how long it will take.

Confirming coverage

How to check that we cover your address

The best way to confirm coverage is to call. Give the dispatcher your street address or cross streets, and say whether the building is on city water and sewer, a well or a septic tank. That one detail changes the plan, and it is the first thing a plumber wants to know.

Drive times vary with traffic on Interstate 80, weather and the time of day. Silver Springs is about 15 miles south on US 95A, which is roughly a twenty minute drive in normal conditions. Call early in a storm, because winter roads and wind slow everyone down.
