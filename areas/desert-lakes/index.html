Plumbing help in the Desert Lakes community

Desert Lakes is a residential community off Farm District Road and Desert Lakes Drive in Fernley, Nevada 89408. A 2015 announcement from its management company describes 828 single-family homes next to a golf course, with access to Highways 95 and 50.

Common calls

Calls that fit a golf course community

These are general situations, not claims about problems that are common in this community.

Water heater in the garage

Listing sites describe many Desert Lakes homes as having two car garages, and a water heater often sits in one. A garage can get very cold on a winter night in this cold desert climate, so a heater that stops heating is worth a call. See water heater repair for the usual causes.

Hose bibs and outdoor lines

Homes with large yards and fairway views have outdoor faucets. A line that freezes can split inside the wall, and the leak shows up later. Shut off and drain outdoor lines before a hard freeze. If you find damp drywall near one, ask about leak detection.

Slow kitchen and bath drains

A drain that slows over weeks is usually a buildup problem, not a sudden failure. Cleaning it early is cheaper than clearing a full stoppage. Start with drain cleaning and describe which fixtures are slow.

Urgent steps

If water is running right now

These steps limit damage while you wait for a plumber.

Close the main valve

Turn the main shutoff clockwise until it stops. Find it before you need it, because it is often near the water heater or where the line enters the house.

Keep power away from water

Stay out of wet areas near outlets and the panel. Switch off a breaker only if you can reach it with dry hands.

Stop using drains

If a drain is backing up, do not flush or run water. Extra water makes a backup spread.

Move what you can

Lift rugs, boxes and electronics off a wet floor. Take photos for your records before you clean up.

Before you hire

Homeowners association rules and city offices

If your home is in the Desert Lakes homeowners association, read its governing documents before work that changes the outside of the house. We do not know your association's rules, and they can differ by phase. Ask the association or its manager if you are unsure.

For permits, the City of Fernley Building Department answers at 775-784-9900 on weekdays. Water and sewer accounts are handled by Utility Billing at 775-784-9930.
