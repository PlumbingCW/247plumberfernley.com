Urgent situations

Before we arrive: what to do

Help takes about twenty minutes to reach most Silver Springs addresses, so these steps limit damage in the meantime.

Shut off the water

Close the main valve at the house. If you are on a well, also turn off the pump breaker so it does not run while a line is open.

Keep power away from water

Stay out of wet areas near outlets and the panel. Turn off the breaker only if you can reach it dry.

Stop using drains

If sewage is backing up, do not flush or run water. On a septic system, extra water can make it worse.

Give clear directions

Silver Springs has long rural roads. Tell the dispatcher your cross streets, any gate code and a landmark.

Water source

Water in Silver Springs: wells, the mutual water company and what that means

Where your water comes from changes how we diagnose and what we recommend.

Silver Springs Mutual Water Company

The company serves about 1,217 connections and roughly 3,043 people, according to its 2025 data. It draws from three wells, at Lake Street, Idaho Street and Deodar, and runs an arsenic-removal plant to meet the 10 parts per billion limit. If you have a question about water quality, contact the company, and bring its report to your plumber if you need a filter chosen.

Private wells

Properties outside the company service area may have private wells. Pressure tanks, pumps and pitless adapters are part of the plumbing. A well problem can look like a leak, so we check pressure and the pump before opening walls.

Why this matters for repairs

Mineral content and pressure vary between systems. We do not assume your water matches Fernley, and we tailor a softener, filter or water heater choice to what your source delivers.
