Overview

Plumbing help in the old downtown area of Fernley

Central Fernley is the part of town around Main Street, which is US 50 Alternate, with Center Street and US 95A as anchors. The encyclopedia entry for Fernley describes the old downtown area as lying between US 95A and Center Street, where In Town Park now sits.

Fernley was founded in 1904 as a farming and ranching community and incorporated as a city in 2001. Housing subdivisions began replacing farmland in the 1970s. That means homes across the city come from many different eras.

Accuracy note

Why we do not assume the age of your pipes

It is tempting to say older streets mean old pipes. We do not, because we found no record that gives building dates or pipe materials for this area. A house here could be original, remodeled or rebuilt.

If you want to know what you have, start with the steps below. The plumber can look at the cleanout, the shutoff and the visible lines, and a camera shows what is underground.

Do this first

How to find out what your property has

Four steps replace guessing with facts.

Ask the county for the year built

The Lyon County Assessor keeps records for each parcel. Ask for the year built on record, and treat it as a starting point.

Check for permits

The City of Fernley Building Department can tell you whether permits were filed for plumbing work at your address.

Locate the cleanout and shutoff

Find the sewer cleanout and the main water valve. Their location and material tell a plumber a lot before any tool comes out.

Book a camera look if unsure

A sewer camera inspection shows the pipe condition from inside, so you decide with real information.

Local landmarks

Parks, streets and getting to you

In Town Park is at 90 South Center Street, and the Main Street Art Park sits along Main Street. Both are handy landmarks when you give directions on the phone.

Tell the dispatcher your cross streets and whether you are on city water and sewer. If you manage a small commercial building nearby, our commercial plumbing page explains that work.

City offices

City offices and permits

For permits, the City of Fernley Building Department answers at 775-784-9900 on weekdays. Water and sewer accounts are handled by Utility Billing at 775-784-9930.

Ask the Building Department first if you are unsure whether a repair needs a permit. Your plumber can handle the application when one is required.
