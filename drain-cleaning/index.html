A drain that will not clear is an emergency once water starts rising toward the rim or sewage smell fills the house. Our line is open 24/7, and while you wait you can stop running water, keep everyone off wet floors and leave chemical cleaners on the shelf.

Emergency drain cleaning in Fernley means a licensed plumber opens the blockage with a cable machine or a water jet, then checks that the line drains freely before the visit ends. Most clogs sit within a few feet of the fixture, but when several drains back up at once the problem is usually in the main line.

Urgent situations

When a clogged drain becomes an emergency

Not every slow drain needs a midnight call. These four situations do, and each one has a first step you can take before we arrive.

Several fixtures back up together

If the tub gurgles when the toilet flushes, or the floor drain bubbles when the washer drains, the blockage is probably in the main line. Stop using water in the house.

Sewage is coming up

Wastewater in a tub or floor drain carries bacteria. Keep kids and pets out, and do not step into it barefoot. Call right away.

Water is near the rim

Do not flush or run the tap again. Shut off the washing machine and dishwasher so they stop adding to the overflow.

The smell will not clear

A sewer odor that returns after you pour water down a drain can mean a dry trap or a vent problem. A plumber can tell which in minutes.

Method

Snake, cable machine or jetting: how the right tool gets chosen

We start with the lightest tool that will clear the line, then step up only when the symptoms say the first pass will not last.

Hand snake for fixture clogs

A hand-cranked auger reaches a few feet past a sink or tub trap and pulls out hair, soap scum and food. It is the right tool for a single slow fixture and is gentle on older pipe.

Cable machine for longer runs

A motorized cable with cutting heads clears the 2 to 4 inch lines that serve a whole bathroom group or the main line. The heads can chew through light root hairs and grease clogs.

Hydro jetting for buildup

A water jet scours the pipe wall, which matters when grease or scale has narrowed the line and clogs keep returning. See hydro jetting if a cable only opens a small hole.

Kitchen

Kitchen sink clogs in Fernley homes

Kitchen lines are the most common reason people call us, because every meal sends fats and soft solids down a narrow drain.

Grease and food solids

Cooled grease coats the pipe a little more with every dish load until a wad of food catches. Hot water alone moves it down the line, where it hardens again.

Disposal and dishwasher link

The dishwasher drain ties into the disposal or the sink tailpiece. A clog there backs dishwater into the sink, and a jammed garbage disposal can look the same.

Double bowls that drain unevenly

If one bowl drains and the other holds water, the blockage usually sits at the tee below the sink, a short cable run away.

Lines that clog every few weeks

Repeat clogs point to a belly in the pipe or heavy buildup. A camera look settles it faster than another cabling pass.

Showers, tubs and floor drains

Bathroom drains fail in a few predictable ways.

  • Hair is the leading cause of a slow shower. A hand tool pulls it out in a few minutes without disturbing the trap.
  • A tub that fills with shower water and drains slowly after the toilet flushes points to a shared branch line, not the tub itself.
  • Basement and garage floor drains often carry only occasional water, so debris sits in the trap until a heavy wash day pushes it back up.
  • A drain line that leaks behind a wall can mimic a clog. If a damp spot shows up on the ceiling below a bathroom, mention it when you call.
Cost factors

What changes the price of a drain cleaning visit

The price depends on how far the blockage sits from the opening, which size of line is involved and whether it is a single fixture or the main line. After-hours work and access problems, such as a cleanout buried under landscaping, also move the number.

We give you a clear price before work starts, and a camera inspection is offered when the cause is unclear. If a cable machine cannot open the line, we tell you why and what the next option costs before we continue. A recurring clog is the one case where a second look is cheaper than a third cabling visit.

Drain care that suits Fernley homes

Fernley is a cold desert town with water that moves through the same pipes all year, so scale and grease do the slow work. Run hot water for a few seconds after greasy dishes, use drain screens in showers and keep wipes out of toilets, since wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does.

Skip store-bought chemical openers. They can sit in a stopped line and burn through pipe joints, and they put a plumber's hands at risk when the line is opened. If you want to check your own water first, the City of Fernley posts annual Consumer Confidence Reports on its Utility Billing page.
