The fastest way to reach a plumber

Call (855) 575-2890. The line is open 24 hours a day, every day, including holidays. Tell the dispatcher what is happening, give your address and your best phone number, and we will schedule a plumber.

This site has no contact form, because a call gets a faster answer. If you cannot call, email contact@247plumberfernley.com and we will reply as soon as we can. Same-day appointments are offered when available.

Address and hours

Fernley 24/7 Plumbing is located at 460 W Main St, Fernley, NV 89408. We dispatch plumbers from Fernley to the city, Silver Springs and nearby areas. Phone lines are open 24/7.

The map below shows our address. It is a dispatch location, not a showroom, so call before you stop by.

What to have ready when you call

A few details speed up the visit. Your address and cross streets, what fixtures are affected, whether water or sewage is involved and whether you have shut off the main. If it is a water heater, say whether it is gas or electric.

For a business, tell us the type of building. For rental properties, tell us who can give access. See our services for the types of work we handle.
