Urgent situations

Before you touch a jammed disposal

Safety comes first with a disposal. These steps protect your hands.

Cut power first

Switch the disposal off at the wall switch and unplug it under the sink or turn off its breaker. A disposal can start without warning.

Never reach inside

Use tongs or pliers to remove an item you can see. Your fingers should stay out of the drain.

Use the hex key

Many units have a hex socket on the bottom. Turning it with the wrench frees a jam on many units.

Press the reset button

A tripped red button on the bottom restores power after an overload. If it trips again right away, call.

Causes

Why disposals jam and fail

Fibrous food such as celery, corn husks and onion skins wraps around the grinder. Bones, fruit pits and utensils can stop it. Grease coats the drain and traps scraps downstream. Running too little water leaves food in the line.

Run cold water before, during and after use, and feed scraps slowly. Avoid grease and coffee grounds in quantity. These habits cut both repair calls and drain clogs.

Repair or replace the disposal

A jam or a tripped reset is a simple repair. A leaking housing means the unit has failed, because the metal case cannot be patched. A unit that is many years old and jams often is also near the end of its life.

We confirm which category it falls in and give a clear price before work starts. A replacement can include a new flange, mounting and power cord. For related kitchen drain work, see faucet repair.

Dishwasher link

Disposals and the dishwasher

The dishwasher drain hose usually connects to the disposal or the sink tailpiece. A clog at that connection can send dishwater into the sink, which looks like a disposal problem but is really a drain issue.

We check the knockout plug on a new disposal, since an unremoved plug blocks the dishwasher drain. If you recently installed a disposal and the dishwasher stopped draining, mention it.

Cost factors

What affects the price of garbage disposal repair

The price depends on whether the unit can be repaired, the horsepower of a replacement, the condition of the drain behind it and the time of the call.

You get a clear price before work starts. We explain the options, repair and replace, with what each costs.
