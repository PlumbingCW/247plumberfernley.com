A faucet that will not shut off, or a supply line spraying under the sink, can flood a kitchen in minutes. Our line is open 24/7. Close the shutoff valve under the sink by turning it clockwise, and if the valve will not work, shut off the main and call.

Faucet repair in Fernley covers dripping spouts, worn cartridges and washers, leaking handles, failing supply lines and stuck shutoff valves. When a faucet is corroded or discontinued, we explain whether a replacement makes more sense than another repair.

Urgent situations

A faucet emergency: what to do first

Under-sink leaks can run unseen. These steps limit damage.

Close the fixture shutoff

Under the sink, turn the small oval-handled valves clockwise. If they are stuck, do not force them.

Close the main if needed

A spraying line with no working shutoff means the main valve has to be closed. Turn it fully clockwise.

Dry the cabinet

Pull out items, wipe water and run a fan. Standing water swells wood and feeds mold.

Check the ceiling below

If the faucet is on an upper floor, check for water coming through the ceiling underneath.

Diagnosis

Where faucets leak and what fixes them

Where the water shows up tells us what to replace.

Where it leaksCauseFix
From the spout when offWorn washer, cartridge or seatReplace the cartridge or washer and seat.
At the handleWorn O-ring or packing nutReplace the O-ring, tighten the nut.
At the base of the faucetLoose mounting or a failed gasketTighten or reseal.
Under the sink at a connectionWorn supply line or loose nutReplace the line or tighten the nut.
From the supply valvePacking or the valve itselfRepack or replace the valve.

Single-handle, two-handle and pull-down faucets use different parts.

Decision

Repair or replace the faucet

A faucet with a worn cartridge is cheap to repair. One with a corroded body, a broken finish or parts that are no longer sold may be better replaced.

We tell you what the part would cost against what a new faucet costs, and we confirm the price before work starts. If the supply valves are old and stuck, replacing them at the same time avoids a second visit. See toilet repair for other fixture work.

Local conditions

Freeze damage to outdoor faucets

January lows near 23 degrees on average mean exterior spigots can freeze if a hose is left attached. Water trapped in the pipe expands and can split the pipe inside the wall, where you will not see it until the thaw.

Disconnect hoses in fall and use an insulated cover. If a spigot drips inside the wall after a freeze, close the main and see burst pipe repair.

The price depends on the faucet brand, how easy the parts are to find, whether the shutoff valves work and the time of the call.
