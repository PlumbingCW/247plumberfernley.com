A burst pipe can release hundreds of gallons in an hour, so act first and call second. Our line is open 24/7. Find the main shutoff and turn it fully closed, then open a low faucet to drain the lines and call us.

Burst pipe repair in Fernley means stopping the water, finding every split, replacing the damaged section and testing the line before water goes back on. After a freeze, a pipe that burst in one place often split in others, so the whole line is checked.

Urgent situations

The first ten minutes after a pipe bursts

Water damage grows fast. These steps are safe to take while you wait for a plumber.

Shut off the main

Turn the main valve clockwise until it stops. If you cannot find it or it will not turn, call the 24/7 line and tell us.

Cut power if water is near it

Water around outlets or the panel is dangerous. Turn off power at the breaker only if you can reach it dry, and stay out of standing water.

Open a low faucet

Draining the system lowers pressure and reduces what spills from the break. Open the lowest cold tap and the hot lines.

Start containing the water

Move belongings, put towels and buckets down, and take photos. Photos help with insurance.

Causes

Why pipes burst in northern Nevada

Fernley is a cold desert with January lows around 23 degrees on average, and colder nights are common. Water expands when it freezes, which builds pressure between the ice and a closed faucet. The pipe often splits where the ice is, but the water only escapes when it thaws.

Pipes in unheated garages, attics, exterior walls and crawlspaces are the usual victims. Corrosion, old fittings and high water pressure can also cause a burst in warm months.

Process

What a repair visit looks like

A good repair covers more than the split you can see.

Stop and assess

We confirm the main is closed, check for more than one break and look at the condition of the nearby pipe. A frozen line can have several splits.

Repair or replace

A short split in copper can be cut out and replaced. Older or brittle pipe may be better replaced for a longer run. See repiping if the whole line is the problem.

Test and restore

We pressure test, open the valve slowly and check every joint. Then we explain what to do about drying and any damage.

Prevention

Preventing the next burst

Prevention is cheaper than a flood. These habits work for Fernley winters.

Insulate exposed pipes

Foam sleeves on pipes in garages and attics slow heat loss. They do not replace heat, though.

Know your main shutoff

Locate it before an emergency and test it once a year so the valve is not stuck.

Let faucets drip in a hard freeze

A small drip relieves pressure between ice and the faucet. It is a short-term measure on exposed lines.

Fix leaks early

A small drip can signal a failing joint. See leak detection for how we trace hidden problems.

Cost factors

What affects the price of burst pipe repair

The price depends on the pipe material, how many breaks there are, how easy the pipe is to reach and the hour of the call. A split in an open garage wall is a different job from one behind a finished ceiling.

You get a clear price before work starts. We explain what the repair covers, what is separate, such as drywall and drying, and what your insurer is likely to ask for. Same-day service is offered when available.
