When the main sewer line stops, every drain in the house is affected, and the lowest fixture is where wastewater shows up. Our line is open 24/7. Until the plumber arrives, stop running water, skip the toilets and keep children and pets away from any wet floor.

Sewer line cleaning in Fernley means a plumber opens the line with a heavy cable machine or a jet, then runs a camera to confirm the pipe is clear. Cleaning is the right call when the pipe is sound and the blockage is grease, paper, roots or debris, and it is a different job from a repair.

Warning signs

Signs the main line has stopped

A stopped sewer line gets worse every time water goes down a drain. Watch for these four signs.

Multiple drains slow at once

Showers, sinks and the washer all drain poorly together. The shared main line is the likely site.

Gurgling from other fixtures

Flushing a toilet makes the tub bubble. Air is being pushed back through traps because the line is blocked downstream.

Water at the lowest drain

A basement or garage floor drain overflows when the washer drains. That is wastewater with nowhere else to go.

Sewer smell outside

A strong odor near the cleanout or along the line can mean the pipe is full or leaking. Call before using more water.

Process

How we clean a main sewer line

We locate the main cleanout first, usually a capped pipe near the foundation or in the yard. The cap is opened carefully because a full line can push wastewater out. A heavy cable with a cutting head then travels the line, breaking up the blockage and pulling debris back.

When grease or scale is the cause, a jet follows the cable so the pipe wall is scoured instead of only opened. See hydro jetting for how that works. A camera pass at the end shows whether the line is clean, damaged or offset, and you can see the screen.

When cleaning is not enough

If the camera shows a collapsed pipe, a belly that holds water or a joint that has pulled apart, cleaning gives short relief at best. We tell you what the picture shows and what the next step would cost before we do anything more.

If the line is sound and just dirty, a periodic cleaning is the honest answer. For homes with tree roots near the line, a camera look every so often is cheaper than a repeat emergency.

Where we work

Prevention

Things that make a main line clog more often

Flushed wipes, even ones labeled flushable, are a leading cause. Grease poured down a kitchen drain cools and builds up. Roots find small gaps in joints and grow into a mat.

Fernley has cold winters with freeze and thaw cycles, and soil movement can shift joints over the years. If your cleanout is easy to reach, the next visit is faster and cheaper. Keep it clear of landscaping and mark it for the plumber.

Cost factors

What affects the price of sewer line cleaning

The price depends on the line length, the pipe diameter, whether a jet is needed after the cable and where the cleanout sits. After-hours calls cost more because of the dispatch.

You get a clear price before work starts. A camera verification is included in the plan we discuss so you can see the result, and we tell you before any repair is added.
