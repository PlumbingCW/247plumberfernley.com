A water heater that is leaking from the tank cannot be repaired, and every hour it leaks adds damage. Our line is open 24/7. Close the cold supply valve above the heater, turn off power or gas and call to schedule a replacement.

Water heater replacement in Fernley means choosing a unit that fits your household, removing the old one with you, setting the new one with correct venting, supply lines and a relief valve and testing everything before we leave. We explain the options in plain terms and give a clear price before work starts.

Urgent situations

Signs you need a replacement, not a repair

Four signs that point to replacing the tank.

Water around the tank body

A leak from the tank itself means the steel liner has failed. No part will fix it.

Rust in the hot water

Rusty hot water, especially with no rust in the cold, can mean the tank is corroding from the inside.

Repeated repairs

A second or third repair in a short span usually means the unit is near the end of its life.

Not enough hot water

A heater that no longer keeps up may be undersized or filled with sediment. A larger or more efficient unit can fix it.

Options

Choosing a replacement unit

Size, fuel and space decide the right unit. We help match the choice to the house.

Storage tank, gas or electric

The most common choice. Sizing depends on how many people use hot water and when. Gas units use a flue, so venting must be checked.

Tankless

Heats water as it flows, which can save space. It needs adequate gas or electric capacity. See tankless water heater for details.

Hybrid and heat pump models

These electric units use less power but need clearance and airflow. They may fit some homes and not others.

Process

What happens on replacement day

Most replacements finish in a single visit when the new unit is in stock and the install is straightforward.

Shut off and drain

We close the supply, turn off power or gas and drain the old tank.

Remove the old unit

The old tank is disconnected and removed from its spot.

Set the new heater

The new tank is placed on a pan with proper support, then piped, vented and fitted with a relief valve line.

Test and show you

We fill the tank, check for leaks, start it and walk you through the controls and the shutoff valves.

Permits and inspections

According to the Nevada State Contractors Board, permits and inspections are required for new and replacement water heaters. A contractor who skips them can be disciplined under state law. We tell you up front how the permit is handled for your job.

The City of Fernley Building Department and Code Enforcement can answer questions at 775-784-9900 on weekdays, and its Online Permit Center is at cityoffernley.org. Ask for the permit and inspection to be recorded for your home.

Local conditions

Replacing a heater in a cold desert home

Fernley's cold winters raise the demand for hot water, and garage installs can be hard on a tank when temperatures drop. A unit in an unheated garage works harder, so insulation and a drain pan matter.

If your current heater is in a garage, ask about moving it to a protected space or adding a blanket and insulated pipe. For related repairs on the existing unit, see water heater repair. A new unit is also a good moment to check shutoff valves and the expansion tank.
